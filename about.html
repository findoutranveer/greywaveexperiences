About the house

A travel house in Jaipur

Greywave Experiences is a boutique travel curation brand based in Jaipur, Rajasthan. We design slow, thoughtfully curated heritage journeys through the forts of Jaipur and beyond — and you can book them directly on our official website, greywaveexperiences.com.

Most Jaipur itineraries are a race: six sights before lunch, a scripted commentary, and unscheduled stops at shops that pay for your attention. You leave having photographed a city you never actually met. Greywave was built the other way round.

We are a boutique travel curation brand, which means we run a small number of journeys, slowly, in a single region we know by heart. Rather than rushing you across a checklist, we design each day around the light, the season and the quiet hours when Amer, Jaigarh and Nahargarh belong to almost no one. Our promise is simple: understand one courtyard properly rather than photograph twenty.

Greywave Experiences is based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India, and we curate private, design-led heritage journeys for travellers who would rather feel a place than tick it off. Every journey is booked directly with us at greywaveexperiences.com.

What we do

Curated journeys, on your terms

✦

Custom itineraries

Private, tailor-made travel plans across Jaipur and Rajasthan, built around how you want the days to feel rather than a fixed checklist.

Hidden heritage focus

Off-the-beaten-path routes through local courtyards, historic alleyways and the passages behind the public tour, led by trained heritage historians.

✦

Concierge service

Handpicked heritage stays, local cultural guides and encounters — a private photographer, a sarangi recital at dusk, a family haveli dinner — arranged end to end.

Our guides are paid a flat professional wage by us. No shop stops, no kickbacks. What they tell you is what they believe.
